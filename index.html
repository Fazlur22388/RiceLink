About RiceConnect

RiceConnect is Sri Lanka's premier digital platform designed to bridge the gap between rice farmers and buyers. Our mission is to empower local farmers by providing them with direct access to markets while ensuring buyers receive the freshest, highest quality rice at fair prices.

We understand the challenges faced by Sri Lankan farmers in reaching potential buyers and getting fair compensation for their hard work. Through our innovative platform, we eliminate middlemen, reduce costs, and create sustainable relationships between producers and consumers.
